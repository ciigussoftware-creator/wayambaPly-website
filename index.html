Customer questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Plywood

Find answers about Wayamba Ply products, plywood quality, prices, wholesale orders and factory information.

What is W.B.P plywood?

W.B.P plywood uses water-resistant bonding designed to withstand humid and demanding conditions. It is commonly used for construction, furniture and applications requiring strong veneer bonding.

Where is Wayamba Ply located?

The Wayamba Ply factory is located in Handugala, Pothuhera, Kurunegala, Sri Lanka.

Is Wayamba Ply plywood ITI tested?

Yes. Wayamba Ply's non-coated plywood was independently tested by the Industrial Technology Institute under test report SS 2510125.

What plywood thicknesses are available?

Wayamba Ply offers plywood in 9 mm, 12 mm, 15 mm and 18 mm thicknesses. Available board sizes and stock may vary, so please contact us to confirm current availability before placing an order.

How can I request plywood prices?

Customers can contact Wayamba Ply by telephone or email to request current plywood prices, wholesale quotations and product availability.

Can customers buy plywood wholesale?

Yes. Customers can purchase plywood from Wayamba Ply in both wholesale and retail quantities. Contact us to discuss prices, availability and order requirements.
